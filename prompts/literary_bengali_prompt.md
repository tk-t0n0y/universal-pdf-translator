# Literary Translation Guidelines (Target: Bengali)

## Style & Diction
- **Register**: Standard Colloquial Bengali (মান্য চলিত বাংলা).
- **Prose Style**: Elegant, expressive, and grammatically precise literary prose.
- **Sentence Flow**: Complex compound sentences may be rendered into clear, cohesive sentences preserving complete meaning and natural rhythm.

---

## Directives
1. **Zero Omission**: Translate every concept, metaphor, and supporting clause. Do not summarize or skip content.
2. **Vocabulary**: Employ rich, context-appropriate vocabulary suited for thoughtful non-fiction and literature.
3. **Terminology**: Retain proper nouns and specialized technical terms with their original spelling in parentheses on first mention.
4. **Punctuation**: Conclude every declarative sentence with a dari (।), interrogative with (?), and exclamatory with (!) to maintain accurate sentence parsing.
